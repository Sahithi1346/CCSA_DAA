ALGORITHMIC FOUNDATIONS

Greedy Choice vs Backtracking

Explore how Design and Analysis of Algorithms principles ensure strict meritocracy and optimal preference satisfaction in engineering admissions.

Greedy Priority Allocation

Merit-First Ascending Rank Order

Candidates are sorted strictly by EAMCET rank (Rank 1 first). The algorithm greedily assigns the candidate’s highest available preference where the candidate satisfies the category closing rank and seat capacity.

Time: O(N log N + N · K) • Space: O(C · B + N)

Backtracking Constraint Search

Recursive State-Space Exploration

Constructs a candidate decision tree. If capacity conflicts or reservation quota bounds block allocation, it backtracks, restores seat counts, and explores alternative preference paths to maximize global welfare.

Time: O(K^N pruned via cutoff bounds) • Space: O(N)
STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How Seat Allocation Works

A seamless 4-step workflow modeled directly on the official Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TSCHE) counselling protocol.

01

Choice-Filling

Students submit prioritized college and branch preferences (Preferences 1 through 5) based on their academic interests.

02

Merit & Cutoff Verification

Student EAMCET Rank is compared against historical 2021 Final Phase closing cutoffs for their category (OC, BC-A/B/C/D/E, SC, ST, EWS).

03

Algorithmic Allocation

Greedy or Backtracking engine processes candidates, dynamically evaluating seat matrix availability and locking valid choices.

04

Allotment Orders

Students receive provisional allotment results with printable verification slips including college details and security hash.
